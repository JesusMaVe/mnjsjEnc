# SecureMessage - Design System

## Brand

- **Product**: SecureMessage - Messaging app with cryptographic integrity
- **Purpose**: Demonstrate cybersecurity concepts (integrity, encryption, digital signatures)
- **Users**: Students in cybersecurity class, 1:1 messaging pairs
- **Tone**: Professional, clean, trustworthy, modern

## Typography

- **Display**: Plus Jakarta Sans (Bold 700)
- **Body**: Inter (Regular 400)
- **Line length**: 65-75ch max

## Components

- ConnectionForm: Card with inputs and button
- ChatRoom: Message list with header and input area
- MessageItem: Message bubble with status badge and validate button

## Status Badges

- No validado: Red background, white text
- Validado: Green background, white text
